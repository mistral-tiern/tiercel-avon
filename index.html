Mistral Tiern

A Death on the Beach — A 5e-Compatible Investigation Module

A blue tiefling is found dead on the beach, ruled a suicide by the Royal Watch. Someone doesn't believe it. Sixty-four districts, a noble house built on embezzlement, and a second-in-command with his own reasons to want a war — the truth is buried under a city that runs on titles, temples, and favors owed.

Party size 3–6, levels 3–5. Open investigation, not a linear crawl. Two 3–4 hour sittings.
